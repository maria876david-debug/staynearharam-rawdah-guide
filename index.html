Visiting the Rawdah — A Simple Guide for Pilgrims

The Rawdah is the small, blessed area inside Masjid an-Nabawi in Madinah, between the Prophet's ﷺ chamber and his pulpit. The Prophet ﷺ said: "What is between my house and my pulpit is a garden from the gardens of Paradise." (Sahih al-Bukhari 1196; Sahih Muslim 1391)

How do you book a Rawdah visit?

Entry is by permit through the official Nusuk app. In simple words: install Nusuk, create your account, open the Rawdah visit section, choose an available day and time slot, and keep the permit ready on your phone. Slots are limited and fill quickly, so check the app regularly — and always follow the current instructions inside the app, because the process can change.

What should you do inside?

Time inside is short. If there is space, pray two rak'ahs, make dua, and send salutations. Keep it brief so others can enter, stay calm in the crowd, and follow the officials' directions at all times.

If no slot is available: keep checking the app — new slots appear. And remember, praying anywhere in Masjid an-Nabawi carries great reward; the Rawdah is a blessed extra, not a required rite of Umrah.
